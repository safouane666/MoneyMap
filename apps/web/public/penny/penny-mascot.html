Penny

A calm gold coin with a slow indigo orbit. Penny has no arms or legs: the orbit shows what she is doing. It glides when she is at rest, speeds up when she thinks, rocks when she says hello, and dims when she sleeps. Ten looping states, one rig, one palette.
